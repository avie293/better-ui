## 1.0.0 - 2026-11-12

| Palworld | Requires                       |
|----------|--------------------------------|
| 1.0.5    | UE4SS Experimental (Palworld)  |

### Added
- Added a settings window, opened through the new "HUD Layout" entry in the pause menu
- Added HUD presets: Clean, Minimal, Vanilla and Custom
- Added settings for every HUD element: show, hide or auto, opacity, size, position, color and bar color
- Added an auto mode that shows health after taking damage, hunger when low, the Pal party when switching Pals and the weapon when switching or shooting
- Added menu themes with seven styles and a free choice of accent, background, text and highlight colors
- Added an option to hide decorative dots, flares and patterns in menus
- Added a size setting for the HUD and menu spacing that keeps every part of the HUD at its screen edge
